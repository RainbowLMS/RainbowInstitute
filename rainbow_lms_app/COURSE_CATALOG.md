## Course design notes

- Native courses contain four lesson sections and a five-question assessment unless configured otherwise.
- Native completion requires the passing score and correct answers to all critical items.
- Supplied interactive courses retain their own detailed module, examination, practical evaluation, and certificate flow.
- Course warnings identify when internal training does not replace licensing, external certification, medical clearance, fit testing, competent-person decisions, or field authorization.
